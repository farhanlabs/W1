Printer Setup Guide

Welcome to our free independent guide. If you recently purchased a new device, it is important to set up brother printer correctly to avoid offline errors. Whether you need a basic wired connection or a complete brother printer setup for your home office, follow our easy, step-by-step instructions below.

1. Brother Printer Wi-Fi Setup

Connecting your device to your local network is the first step. If you want to set up wifi on brother printer, the easiest method is using the printer's LCD control panel.

  • Press the 'Settings' or 'Menu' button on your device.
  • Navigate to 'Network' and then select 'WLAN'.
  • Choose the Setup Wizard.
  • Select your home network (SSID) and enter the password.

Once connected, the wireless symbol will appear. You are now ready to add brother wifi printer to any device on the exact same network for seamless wireless printing.

2. Connect Brother Printer to Computer (Windows)

Are you trying to figure out how to connect brother printer to computer? Windows users can complete this process in just a few minutes. To successfully add brother printer to computer, ensure that your PC and printer are either connected via a USB cable or sharing the same Wi-Fi network.

  • Open your Windows 'Settings' and go to 'Devices' (or 'Bluetooth & devices').
  • Click on 'Printers & scanners'.
  • Click the 'Add a printer or scanner' button.
  • Windows will scan for available devices. Select your model from the list to finish the installation.

3. Add Brother Printer To Mac

For Apple users, the process is slightly different. To add brother printer to mac, your macOS needs to detect the device on the local network (via AirPrint) or via a direct USB connection.

  • Click the Apple logo in the top-left corner and select 'System Settings' (or System Preferences).
  • Scroll down and click on 'Printers & Scanners'.
  • Click the 'Add Printer, Scanner, or Fax' button (the small '+' icon).
  • Select your device from the Default tab list and click 'Add'. Your Mac will automatically configure the correct drivers.
