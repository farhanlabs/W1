Welcome to PCConnectHub, accessible at pcconnecthub.com. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and disclosed when you visit our website.

PCConnectHub is an independent educational technology publication providing information about computers, networking, operating systems and device troubleshooting. This policy applies to information processed through our website.

Important information

PCConnectHub does not require visitors to create an account to read its educational content. However, our hosting provider, analytics tools, advertising partners or other third-party services may process certain technical information, depending on the services enabled on the website.

1. Information We Collect

The information processed through PCConnectHub depends on how you use the website and which services are enabled.

  • Technical information: Your IP address, browser type, operating system, device information and approximate location derived from an IP address may be processed by hosting or security services.
  • Usage information: Pages visited, visit timestamps, referring pages and interactions may be recorded by server logs or analytics tools, if enabled.
  • Information you provide: If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include.

We do not intentionally request payment card details or government identification numbers through the website. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for your inquiry.

2. Log Files

Our hosting provider or website security services may maintain server logs for security, troubleshooting, performance monitoring and operational purposes. Depending on the configuration, logs may contain IP addresses, browser details, request timestamps, referring URLs and requested pages.

Log retention periods depend on the hosting provider, applicable settings and legitimate operational requirements. We do not promise a specific retention period unless one is expressly stated.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Depending on the features enabled, PCConnectHub or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, measure website usage, protect against abuse or deliver advertising.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of some website features. Where required by applicable law, consent will be requested before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

4. Advertising and Monetization

PCConnectHub may use third-party advertising services, including Google advertising products, if and when these services are enabled.

Advertising providers may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses or other information, subject to their own policies and applicable law, to serve, measure or personalize advertisements and prevent invalid activity.

The information processed and the advertising choices available depend on the providers and settings in use. We will update this policy if our advertising practices change materially.

For information about Google's data practices, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

5. Third-Party Services and External Links

Our website may link to third-party websites, documentation, videos or online resources. We may also rely on external providers for hosting, security, analytics or advertising.

When you visit an external website, that provider's privacy policy and terms may apply. PCConnectHub does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties and encourages you to review their policies before sharing personal information.

6. Data Security and Retention

We and our service providers may use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information processed through the website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including security, legal compliance, dispute resolution and operational needs, subject to applicable law.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.
